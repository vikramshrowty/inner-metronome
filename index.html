Accuracy (±35ms)
How it works:
  • Dropped Click Isolation: On dropped clicks, the speaker produces 0 sound. Any transient detected by the microphone is guaranteed to be your hands clapping—eliminating speaker-to-mic bleed.
  • Offset Calculation: $\text{Error} = (T_{\text{detected}} - \Delta_{\text{calibrated}}) - T_{\text{scheduled}}$. Negative values mean you rushed; positive values mean you dragged.
  • Jitter vs Bias: Bias shows if you lean early or late. Jitter ($\sigma$) measures your rhythm consistency regardless of bias. Drummers typically aim for $\sigma \le 18\text{ ms}$.
